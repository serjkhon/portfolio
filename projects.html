My own astrophysics Python library to calculate orbital mechanics (Lidov-Kozai effects, two-body/three-body problem utilities). I am fully aware that astropy exists and is much better, but I wanted to make it anyway. Named in honor of IU Bloomington, both my favorite city in the world and alma mater.
