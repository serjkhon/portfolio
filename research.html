Lidov-Kozai Oscillations: A Computational Replication Study wip

Beginner numerical model of Lidov-Kozai oscillations in hierarchical triple systems. The goal is to validate the classical mechanism first, then gradually extend the model toward compact-object binaries near massive black holes. This project is heavily inspired by the work of Dr. Cristobal Petrovich at IU's astronomy department.
